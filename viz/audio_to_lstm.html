Turning an echo into an LSTM sequence for classification

The recurrent core reads a sequence of vectors $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_T$, one per timestep. What one $x_t$ contains — and how many steps $T$ there are — is a windowing choice made before the LSTM. Three definitions, same ultrasonic echo return:

Last state — $z = h_T$. Simplest; trusts the final state to hold the whole-sequence summary.
Mean / max pool — $z = \operatorname{pool}_t\, h_t$. Robust when the cue is spread across time, not just at the end.
Attention pool — $z = \sum_t \alpha_t\, h_t$, learned weights $\alpha_t$. Concentrates on the most informative timesteps.
BiLSTM — $z = [\,h_T^{\rightarrow};\, h_0^{\leftarrow}\,] \in \mathbb{R}^{128}$. Concatenates a forward and backward pass, so $z$ sees past and future.

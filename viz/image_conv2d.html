One 3×3×3 filter slides over the (3×32×32) RGB input. Its three kernels are applied to R, G and B; each produces a partial sum, the three are added together with the single per-filter bias, and the result becomes one cell of the output feature map. Repeat over every position → one 30×30 map; repeat over all 32 filters → a (32×30×30) output volume. Drag filter to change the weights, and K / S / P to see how the output size formula responds. Use step to advance one position at a time.
